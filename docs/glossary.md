# Glossary

- **Amplitude:** a complex weight for a basis state; programmer translation: a value whose squared magnitude becomes sampling probability.
- **Basis state:** one concrete bitstring such as `010`.
- **Bitstring:** a sequence of binary decisions.
- **Qubit:** a quantum two-state system; programmer translation: one binary decision represented by amplitudes until measurement.
- **Superposition:** a state with multiple nonzero amplitudes.
- **Phase:** complex-number direction that controls later interference.
- **Gate:** a reversible state transformation.
- **Unitary:** a norm-preserving reversible matrix.
- **Measurement:** sampling a classical result from amplitudes.
- **Shot:** one circuit execution and measurement.
- **Hamiltonian:** a physics operator; here, a quantum-compatible representation of an optimization energy.
- **Cost Hamiltonian:** the operator encoding QUBO energy.
- **Mixer:** an operation that moves amplitude between candidate states.
- **QUBO:** quadratic unconstrained binary optimization.
- **Ising model:** a spin-variable representation closely related to QUBO.
- **QAOA:** a parameterized cost/mixer circuit with a classical outer optimization loop.
- **Ansatz:** a parameterized circuit family.
- **Gamma:** cost-layer parameter.
- **Beta:** mixer parameter.
- **Depth p:** number of cost/mixer repetitions.
- **Expectation value:** average measured energy over a distribution.
- **Classical optimizer:** CPU algorithm updating circuit parameters.
- **Transpilation:** rewriting an abstract circuit for hardware connectivity.
- **Noise:** unwanted operation or measurement error.
